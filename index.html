Four simulators, four jobs

Wokwi

Runs your Arduino UNO and ESP32 code on a simulated board, in the browser. No account needed to run a project.

Best for: Testing code and wiring before you touch hardware.

wokwi.com
Tinkercad Circuits

A breadboard on screen with real parts, an Arduino UNO that runs your code, and a virtual multimeter. Free Autodesk account.

Best for: Practising the whole build-and-measure routine of a chapter.

www.tinkercad.com/circuits
Falstad CircuitJS

An animated circuit simulator: you see current flowing and voltages moving on a scope. Runs in the browser with no account.

Best for: Seeing what a capacitor, a diode or a coil is doing, moment by moment.

www.falstad.com/circuit
LTspice

The free circuit simulator from Analog Devices used across industry. Download the program once, then open the .asc file and press Run.

Best for: Frequency plots, sweeping a part's value, and exact waveforms.

www.analog.com/ltspice
